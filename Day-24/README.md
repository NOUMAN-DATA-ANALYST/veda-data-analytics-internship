# Day 24: Excel PivotTable Basics — Data Analytics Internship

## 📌 Overview
This project focuses on summarizing raw transactional sales data using **Excel PivotTables**. By transforming static datasets into dynamic analytical views, key business insights were extracted regarding sales performance across product categories, geographic regions, and monthly timelines.

---

## 🎯 Objectives & Key Deliverables
- Clean and prepare raw sales data (formatting, data types, and structural alignment).
- Convert raw dataset into a dynamic Excel Table (`Ctrl + T`) to ensure scalable data range handling.
- Construct 3 dedicated PivotTables:
  1. **Sales by Category**: Evaluating revenue distribution across product lines.
  2. **Sales by Region**: Analyzing geographic market performance.
  3. **Sales by Month**: Uncovering time-series trends and seasonal patterns.
- Summarize actionable business insights and document core technical interview concepts.

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Category Trends:** **Technology** generates the highest overall revenue, whereas **Office Supplies** records the highest transaction volume.
- **Regional Performance:** The **West Region** leads total sales revenue, while the **South Region** presents growth opportunities as the lowest contributor.
- **Seasonal Analysis:** Sales peak significantly during **Q4 (October – December)**, highlighting strong seasonal and holiday demand dynamics.

---

## ❓ Conceptual Q&A
**Q1: Why use PivotTables?**  
> *Answer:* PivotTables enable fast, dynamic aggregation, grouping, and analysis of large datasets without complex formulas, allowing efficient reporting and business decision-making.

**Q2: What is a PivotTable source range?**  
> *Answer:* A PivotTable source range is the underlying dataset fed into the PivotTable. Using a structured Excel Table (`Ctrl + T`) ensures dynamic automatic expansion whenever new data is appended.
