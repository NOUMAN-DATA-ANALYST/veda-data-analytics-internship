# HR Analytics: Department & Role-Level Workforce Insights

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing organizational HR data to extract key insights regarding headcount distribution, role-level salary structures, and department trends using Microsoft Excel (PivotTables & PivotCharts). 

The goal is to enable HR leadership and business executives to make data-backed decisions on compensation planning, workforce allocation, and organizational restructuring.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Microsoft Excel**: Data Preprocessing, Excel Tables, Dynamic Formulas.
- **PivotTables**: Multi-dimensional aggregation (Department & Job Role breakdown).
- **PivotCharts**: Visual representations (Column Charts, Bar Charts, Donut Charts).

---

## 📊 Key Analysis & Deliverables

### 1. Data Cleaning & Table Structuring
- Converted raw HR dataset into a dynamic **Excel Table** format for seamless querying and visual consistency.
- Standardized data types for metrics such as `Employee ID`, `Department`, `JobRole`, and `MonthlyIncome`.

### 2. PivotTable Summaries
- **Salary Analysis**: Grouped `Department` and `JobRole` to calculate the `Average Monthly Income`.
- **Headcount Analysis**: Aggregated employee counts across departments to identify workforce density.

### 3. Interactive HR Dashboard (PivotCharts)
- **Average Monthly Income by Job Role**: Highlights compensation tiers across different job functions.
- **Headcount by Department**: Displays the distribution of staff across organizational departments.
- **Workforce Distribution by Job Role**: Provides a detailed breakdown of role-level staffing.

---

## 💡 HR Insights & Business Questions Answered

1. **Why use Median Salary instead of Average Salary?**
   - *Insight:* Average salary can be skewed by high-earning executives (outliers). **Median Salary** reflects the true midpoint of payroll, offering a more accurate representation of employee compensation.

2. **What does Department-wise Headcount reveal?**
   - *Insight:* It shows resource deployment density, helping HR optimize recruitment budgets and balance operational workloads across departments.

3. **What is the business impact of Role-Level Breakdown?**
   - *Insight:* Identifying department and role distributions enables company leadership to pinpoint high-cost departments, eliminate payroll anomalies, and refine promotion pipelines.
